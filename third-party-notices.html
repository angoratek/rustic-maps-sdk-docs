Credits for projects this SDK learns from. Where only a design was learned from, no code was copied; where an entry says logic was ported, the derived work is credited and its license text is retained under docs/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/.

  • MapLibre — its open map renderers and how they frame tile scheduling, frustum listing and tile budgeting are the mature prior art this SDK’s renderer seams knowingly re-implement from first principles.
  • OpenStreetMap and its ODbL database — the map-data backbone this SDK renders; the ODbL attribution obligations above are real and are honored in every reference app.
