The MapQuest terms’ imagery-caching ceiling: raster-tile responses may be stored for up to 30 calendar days. Declared here as the provider-specific caching term (PRD §6.7, owner ruling 2026-10-28) so an integrator caching tiles knows the upper bound for this provider; the general cache layer’s tiles default equals this ceiling, enforced by tiles.rs’s lockstep test.
