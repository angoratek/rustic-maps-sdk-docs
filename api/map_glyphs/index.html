Glyph support for the symbol/text pipeline (M9 slice S2): the Mapbox glyph protobuf reader, a byte-bounded range cache and an R8Unorm atlas packer.

The crate is pure byte parsing and CPU-side packing. Its only runtime dependencies are thiserror and the workspace’s dependency-free protobuf wire reader (map-vector-tiles), so it builds on native targets and on wasm32-unknown-unknown alike. Nothing here draws, touches a GPU pipeline or places labels; the renderer’s text pass is slice S3.

§Scope: Latin only

The reader parses whichever codepoints a producer serves, and the atlas packs the Latin ranges first, but there is no shaping, bidirectional ordering or ligature handling. Complex-script text (Arabic, Indic, …) is explicitly out of scope until a shaping dependency is justified.

Entry points: decode_glyph_font_stack turns a fetched glyph range into a GlyphFontStack, GlyphRangeCache retains decoded ranges under a byte and entry ceiling, and pack_glyph_atlas lays glyphs out on one R8Unorm page with per-glyph texture rectangles.
