The line blur, in screen pixels.

§line_offset: Option<Property<f64>>

The distance the line is shifted perpendicular to its path, in screen pixels; a positive value shifts the line to the right of its direction.

§line_gap_width: Option<Property<f64>>

The width of the gap a line is drawn around, in screen pixels; the stroke paints outside the gap on both sides.
