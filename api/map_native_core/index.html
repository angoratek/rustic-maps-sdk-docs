Source
Expand description

The only UniFFI binding crate: objects, records, callback interfaces and the UDL source of truth consumed by Kotlin, Swift, and uniffi-bindgen-react-native; the wasm target uses the hand-checked TS surface verified against the same UDL.

Milestone M4, slice 1 (2026-10-27) freezes the camera/projection contract in src/map_native_core.udl and implements it here by delegating to map-rendering: the anchored camera::MapCamera (screen <-> ground projection, visible tile listing, tile zoom selection), the free projection helpers, and get_sdk_compiled_version. The UDL dictionaries are mirrored by the value structs in records; FFI traits cannot be implemented for foreign-crate types, so binding records convert to and from the geospatial-types and map-rendering types through the explicit record constructors and the projection validation helpers. error::SdkError mirrors the geospatial-types SDKError subset these calls produce. Extended in M4 slice 2 (2026-10-27) with the fit-bounds constructor (camera::MapCamera::fit_bounds, the EdgeInsets record). Extended in M4 slice 3 (2026-10-27) with the async provider surface: provider::MapSdk resolves the implemented geospatial-traits contracts through the map-core ProviderRegistry with one exported [Async] method per call, the provider records and enums mirror geospatial-types, and error::SdkError grows the provider failure variants. Extended in M4 slice 4 (2026-10-27) with the foreign-callback provider injection (foreign_providers): the [Trait, WithForeign] contracts GeocodingProvider and RasterTileProvider that foreign code implements, the MapSdk::register_*_from_foreign methods wrapping them in the foreign_providers adapters onto the native geospatial-traits traits, and the error round-trip From<SdkError> for SDKError. Extended in M6 (2026-10-27) with the navigation wiring (navigation): the [Trait, WithForeign] listener NavigationListener foreign code implements against the map-navigation broadcaster behind the navigation::Navigator (trip start with policy records standing in for the native decision objects, location updates, reroutes, and the read-only state and instruction payloads), the Subscription cancel handle, and the mirror records of the map-navigation policy records standing in for the native decision objects, location updates, reroutes, and the read-only state and instruction payloads), the Subscription cancel handle, and the mirror records of the map-navigation state. Extended in round 8 (2026-10-27) with the native surface wiring (surface_view, the MapView object over map_rendering::SurfaceTileRenderer — lifecycle, overlay list and the per-frame async draw — with the platform_surface lowering of the platform pointer into a wgpu surface, the overlay and handle record mirrors, the two render failure variants of SdkError, and the set_provider_credential call installing the MapQuest providers with an API key) and the runtime executor seam: every exported async method hands its reactor-dependent work (the reqwest provider futures, the surface draw’s tile fetches) to a crate-owned tokio runtime through [runtime::drive], so the foreign executor polls only a runtime-independent handback future. Extended in round 12 (2026-10-29) with the addition-only provider-failure diagnostic seam (provider_diagnostics): every failed foreign-callback provider call records a records::ProviderFailureDiagnostic (classification from records::ProviderFailureClassification, verbatim failure text) on the owning MapSdk’s bounded ring, read back through MapSdk::latest_provider_failure_diagnostics. Extended in the M17 contract slice (2026-10-30) with the engine facades (engines: SearchEngine, RoutingEngine, OfflineManager, Diagnostics, each constructed over an existing MapSdk and delegating verbatim), the id-addressed overlay members on surface_view::MapView (add_overlay/update_overlay/remove_overlay, beside the documented bulk set_overlays), and the Navigator listener/stop verbs (add_navigation_listener’s four-topic composite subscription and stop_navigation). Remaining for later slices: the remaining provider contracts as foreign callbacks, and the RN generation pipeline.

Every exported entry point of this crate (and every callback invocation inside an exported frame) is panic-contained by the uniffi 0.32.2 scaffolding itself; crate::panic_boundary documents that verified containment map, which is why this crate adds no redundant catch_unwind layer of its own — and holds the crate’s unwrap/ expect policy for the FFI-reachable code.

Modules§

camera
The UniFFI-facing anchored camera, wrapping map-rendering’s camera; the validation the core leaves to callers happens on every binding call, so the frozen surface can only ever receive validated camera state.
diagnostics
The diagnostics sink (round 10, OQ6): the SdkDiagnosticEventListener foreign-callback contract foreign code implements to receive the SDK’s structured internal log events, plus the ring-buffer tracing subscriber and the sink’s privacy scrubbing behind MapSdk::subscribe_to_diagnostic_events and MapSdk::flush_recent_diagnostic_events.
engines
The M17 engine facades (addition-only contract slice): typed capability objects constructed over an existing MapSdk.
error
The error model of the native binding surface: a fielded mirror of the geospatial-types SDKError variants the frozen calls can actually produce, carrying their verbatim detail messages across the FFI.
foreign_providers
The foreign-callback provider injection surface (M4 slice 4): the exported callback contracts that foreign code (Kotlin, Swift) implements to serve a provider contract of the SDK, and the adapters that bridge those implementations onto the native geospatial-traits traits behind the map-core [ProviderRegistry].
navigation
The navigation wiring surface (M6): the NavigationListener foreign-callback contract foreign code implements to observe the navigation state, the Navigator driving a native map-navigation session behind it, and the policy records configuring the controller’s step-advance and deviation strategies.
panic_boundary
Panic containment at the FFI edges
platform_surface
The per-platform lowering of the exported crate::records::SurfaceHandle into a wgpu::SurfaceTargetUnsafe: the seam map-rendering’s surface renderer deliberately leaves open. Each supported platform compiles its own guarded branch (macOS on the host, iOS and Android only under their cross targets); every other target refuses at the boundary instead of guessing which pointer to read.
projection
The free projection helpers of the frozen surface, mirroring map-web-bindings’ namespace functions one-to-one, plus the input validation they and the camera calls share.
provider
The UniFFI-facing async provider surface (M4 slice 3): one MapSdk object resolving the implemented geospatial-traits contracts through the map-core ProviderRegistry, one exported async method per contract call mirroring the trait surface one-to-one.
provider_diagnostics
The provider-failure diagnostic seam (round 12, addition-only; the owner ruling of 2026-10-29 on unclassified foreign provider failures): the bounded per-MapSdk ring ProviderFailureDiagnosticsRing recording every failed foreign-callback provider call, read back through MapSdk::latest_provider_failure_diagnostics.
records
The binding-surface records frozen in the UDL contract (src/map_native_core.udl).
runtime
The crate-owned async runtime: the executor seam of the exported [Async] surface.
surface_view
The exported map surface view: the FFI face of the map-rendering surface renderer, attaching a platform window surface, driving its lifecycle and drawing camera-tiled frames with the overlay list over them.
