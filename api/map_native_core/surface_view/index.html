The exported map surface view: the FFI face of the map-rendering surface renderer, attaching a platform window surface, driving its lifecycle and drawing camera-tiled frames with the overlay list over them.

§The view-state locking discipline

draw awaits the tile fetches, so no lock may be held across the await while the exported future must be Send. The view therefore keeps its surface state (renderer, tile scheduler, extent) in a dedicated slot the whole draw owns for its duration: draw takes the session out of the slot under one brief lock, awaits and renders with no lock held, and restores it afterwards — under a second brief lock that also honors a detach racing the draw. The same take-out serves resize, so resize works while a fetch is in flight unless its own brief configure collides with a render in progress. Overlays live in their own lock, taken only to clone the list for the frame. The consequences are documented on the methods (serialized draws, a mid-draw resize surfacing the busy state).
