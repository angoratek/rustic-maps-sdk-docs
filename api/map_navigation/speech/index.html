SDK-owned speech-instruction triggering (round 10, OQ5): when a guidance line is newsworthy enough to speak.

The SDK stays out of synthesis and playback (the PRD non-goal and the §3.4 platform policies hold): this module only decides when a fix is worth a spoken line, and answers with the composed instruction payload’s verbatim speech text — never text this module authored. The decision reuses the display phrase’s existing distance bands so a spoken “in 100 meters” can never disagree with the banner’s shown phrase: the thresholds below are the very bands crate::instruction::DistancePhrase rounds with.

§Emission rules (the named behaviors)

  • Trigger bands are the distance-phrase bands. Fix N crossed below a band’s threshold (in meters) announces that band: metric 10 m, 100 m, 1 km and the immediate half-increment threshold; imperial 100 ft, 1 000 ft, 1 mile and the immediate threshold. Each band announces at most once per step, and only the strictest band crossed in one fix speaks (OsmAnd’s prompt-stage ladder: a fix that jumps two bands announces the nearer stage only).
  • Monotone per step. The band memory resets only on a step advance, so distance jitter over a threshold can never re-announce a band.
  • Minimum inter-utterance spacing. After any line, the next band-triggered line waits for MINIMUM_INTER_UTTERANCE_SPACING_SECONDS (the round-9 platform pacing constant, now SDK-owned). The immediate band overrides it — OsmAnd’s “turn now” stage always plays.
  • Minimum repeat distance. A ManeuverApproach line whose trigger distance lies within MINIMUM_UTTERANCE_REPEAT_DISTANCE_METERS of the previous emission’s trigger distance is suppressed — Organic Maps’ minDistToSayNotificationMeters: two turns announced from distances closer than this read as chatter instead of guidance (and Organic Maps chains “then” where this seam has no “then” authoring; the near band’s own silence is the honest fallback).
  • Transitions always speak. An off-route recovery and an arrival emit unconditionally (the composed payload’s verbatim text) — they are the guidance-resumed and you-have-arrived lines, not pacing candidates, and like every emission they refresh the spacing memory.
  • Every emission carries its identity. Each emitted SpeechUtterance is stamped with a fresh, strictly increasing SpeechUtterance::utterance_id (Ferrostar’s utterance-id mechanism, ours composed at emission) at the single emission site, and the counter survives every reset — so identical text re-emitted is a new identity, and the platform speaker’s dedup and flush decisions stay keyed on its own queue state.
