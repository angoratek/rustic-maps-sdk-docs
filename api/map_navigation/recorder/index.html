Module recorder

Expand description

Opt-in navigation recording for field diagnostics and test debugging.

Round 11, mirroring the reference navigation SDK’s recorder mechanism with our own expressions (inspiration-only, no reference code): a NavigationRecorder implements the per-topic NavigationStateChangeListener surface and additionally records the raw position fixes delivered to the reducer through an explicit NavigationRecorder::record_location_fix channel (the raw fixes are not a listener topic — the listener surface starts downstream of them). Every recording is stamped with a strictly increasing sequence number and, when the clock is available, an instant in seconds since the Unix epoch, and is kept in a bounded ring that silently drops its oldest entry once full — a recorder can never grow unbounded or slow the trip down beyond one short locked section per recorded item.

Recording starts when the recorder is created (opt-in, no-op otherwise: nothing records unless a consumer holds a recorder and subscribes it or feeds it fixes) and stops when the recorder is dropped from every subscription.

Internal-only this round: the frozen UDL contract carries no recorder surface, and the drained entries are held as the plain-data payloads the other navigation modules emit. A cross-FFI wiring or a serialized dump waits for the payload types’ shared serialization shape (an owner question, not decided here).
