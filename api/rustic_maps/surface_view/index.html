The native surface view over rustic-maps-rendering’s surface renderer. The exported map surface view: the FFI face of the rustic-maps-rendering surface renderer, attaching a platform window surface, driving its lifecycle and drawing camera-tiled frames with the overlay list over them.

§The view-state locking discipline

draw awaits the tile fetches, so no lock may be held across the await while the exported future must be Send. The view therefore keeps its surface state (renderer, tile scheduler, extent) in a dedicated slot the whole draw owns for its duration: draw takes the session out of the slot under one brief lock, awaits and renders with no lock held, and restores it afterwards — under a second brief lock that also honors a detach racing the draw. The same take-out serves resize, so resize works while a fetch is in flight unless its own brief configure collides with a render in progress. Overlays live in their own lock, taken only to clone the shared snapshot the frame draws. The consequences are documented on the methods (serialized draws, a mid-draw resize surfacing the busy state).

§Cancellation-safe ownership

The borrowed session and vector-base scheduler move into the spawned frame work, so the restore cannot live on the caller’s future: a caller that drops its future mid-draw would otherwise strand the slot Busy. A [FrameOwnership] guard inside the work restores the borrowed state when the frame ends — normally, on an early error, or while unwinding from a panic — holding Arc handles to the view’s slots so it can write them back. A racing detach wins: the guard drops the session and the slot stays honestly detached. The spawned work always runs to completion on the crate-owned runtime even when the awaiting future is gone, so the slot is recoverable after every way a frame can end.
