Module renderer 

Source
Expand description

GPU-free frame assembly: prepared tiles mapped to drawable quads.

The pure half of the renderer. A Renderer consumes the camera pose, the viewport, and the tiles the crate::tile_scheduler::TileScheduler prepared for the frame, and produces the list of textured quads to draw in normalized world coordinates, including parent-fallback quads for tiles that are not yet pooled.

Quads are projected into per-fragment clip-space vertex arrays: a world span is wrapped around the camera as one unit (never corner by corner) and split into at most one vertex array per world copy it reaches, so spans crossing the antimeridian render contiguously instead of tearing. See [FrameProjection].

Everything in this module is free of wgpu types, so the quad assembly, the fallback texture window math, and the clip-space projection of the quad corners are all unit-testable without a GPU.

Structs§

ClipSpaceTileVertex
One quad corner as consumed by the GPU pipeline: a position in clip space (homogeneous coordinates; the perspective divide happens on the GPU) and the texture coordinates to sample there. repr(C) keeps the field order the vertex buffer layout expects.
Renderer
Quad assembler for one frame: turns prepared tiles into draw items and projects their corners into clip space. Construct it once and reuse it across frames; it holds only the field of view.
TextureWindow
The sub-window of a texture that a quad samples: u grows rightward with the texture columns and v grows downward with the texture rows, so at v = 0 the image’s top row — the northern edge of a tile — is sampled. All components lie in [0, 1].
TileQuad
One textured quad to draw for a frame, in normalized world coordinates.
TileWorldSpan
Normalized-world rectangle one tile occupies: x grows eastward and y grows southward across [0, 1] (web-mercator convention), so the tile grid subdivides the unit square from the north.

Constants§

CLEAR_COLOR_RGBA
Backdrop color behind the map. Opaque black: a tile that never arrives blends into the void instead of flashing white.
VIEWPORT_NORMALIZED_DEVICE_BOUNDS
Normalized-device-coordinate bounds of the viewport in both axes. Quads whose entire projected bounding box lies outside these bounds are culled during assembly.
