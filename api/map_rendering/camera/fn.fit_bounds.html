Expand description

The camera that frames a bounding box inside the viewport’s inset region.

Bounds fitting is top-down and north-up by construction (pitch and bearing zero), matching every leading SDK: perspective fitting is ill-defined and would double-fit against the frustum clip. The zoom is the largest level whose ground scale keeps the whole box inside the inset area, clamped to maximum_zoom_level; the center is the box’s normalized-world midpoint, shifted so its ground point lands on the inset region’s center under asymmetric insets. The whole-box-inside-the-inset-area guarantee holds only for the unclamped fit: when the zoom is clamped (by maximum_zoom_level, or by the minimum zoom for boxes too large for the world), the box overflows the inset region and the center-anchor guarantee is the one that survives. Returns an error when the insets or the maximum zoom are not finite usable values, when the insets are negative or leave no inset area (left + right ≥ viewport width and likewise vertical), or when the box spans no positive world area (non-finite or reversed corners).
