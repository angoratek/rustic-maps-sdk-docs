Module camera 

Source
Expand description

Camera model: geographic center, zoom, bearing, and pitch, projected into a perspective viewport whose ground magnification matches the zoom scale.

The camera is the mapbox-style anchored model: the ground point under MapCamera::center always sits at the viewport center, the camera hovers above it at the altitude implied by the zoom level, and bearing and pitch rotate and tilt the view around that anchor. Projection math runs on f64 matrices because world units reach ~8.6e9 pixels at zoom 24.

DEFAULT_FLY_TO_TRAVEL_SPEED
Default travel speed of the fly-to arc, in screenfulls of the arc per second (speed in the fly-to derivation, from the leading GL JS implementation): the derived duration is the arc length divided by this.
DEFAULT_FLY_TO_ZOOM_OUT_CURVE
Default zoom-out tightness of the fly-to arc (curve, ρ in the fly-to derivation, from the leading GL JS implementation): larger values dip the zoom deeper below the endpoints’ levels as the map travels along a long flight path. The arc also self-limits when the unclamped tightness would dip the zoom past the SDK’s minimum zoom level.
