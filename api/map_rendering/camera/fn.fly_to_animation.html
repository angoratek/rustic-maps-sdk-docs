Expand description

The camera animation that flies between two cameras instead of cutting between them: zoom follows the fly-to arc’s exponential dip — long travels zoom out along the way and zoom back in on arrival, short travels slide almost monotonically — while the center, bearing, and pitch ease normally.

The arc follows the leading GL JS implementation’s derivation, adapted to the SDK’s mercator-anchored camera: the visible span w(s) shrinks and grows hyperbolically along the path in units of the initial viewport span, and the derived duration (absent an override) is the arc length over the travel speed. The outer easing deliberately stays the SDK’s smoothstep (interpolate_camera uses the same), trading the GL JS default cubic- bezier easing for one animation easing story across both pose curves.

Errors report non-finite or unusable input; use CameraAnimation::new for a plain cut-interpolated animation.
