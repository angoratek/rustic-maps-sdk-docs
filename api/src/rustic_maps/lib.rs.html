1//! The UniFFI binding crate of the SDK: objects, records, callback
2//! interfaces and the UDL contract consumed by Kotlin, Swift and
3//! `uniffi-bindgen-react-native`. The WebAssembly target uses a
4//! hand-maintained TypeScript surface checked against the same UDL.
5//!
6//! The crate exposes the anchored [`camera::MapCamera`] and its free
7//! [`projection`] helpers, the async provider surface over
8//! [`provider::MapSdk`], the foreign-callback provider contracts
9//! ([`foreign_providers`]), the navigation controller ([`navigation`]), the
10//! map surface view and its platform pointer lowering ([`surface_view`],
11//! [`platform_surface`]), the simulated-trip seam ([`simulation`]) and the
12//! device-positioning controller ([`positioning`]). The diagnostics surface
13//! ([`diagnostics`], [`provider_diagnostics`]) reports SDK log events and
14//! failed foreign provider calls.
15//!
16//! The UDL dictionaries are mirrored by the value structs in [`records`]:
17//! FFI traits cannot be implemented for foreign-crate types, so binding
18//! records convert to and from the `geospatial-types` and `rustic-maps-rendering`
19//! types through explicit constructors and the `projection` validation
20//! helpers. [`error::SdkError`] mirrors the `geospatial-types` `SDKError`
21//! subset these calls produce.
22//!
23//! Every exported async method hands reactor-dependent work (the `reqwest`
24//! provider futures, the surface draw's tile fetches) to a crate-owned tokio
25//! runtime through [`runtime::drive`], so a foreign executor polls only a
26//! runtime-independent handback future.
27//!
28//! The engine facades ([`engines`]: `SearchEngine`, `RoutingEngine`,
29//! `OfflineManager`, `Diagnostics`) construct typed capability objects over
30//! an existing `MapSdk` and delegate every member to it. The offline routing
31//! seam ([`offline_routing`]) exposes the contract's
32//! `offline_route_from_archive_bytes` function: archive bytes in, a route
33//! out, over the same `rustic-maps-core` implementation the WebAssembly export uses.
34//!
35//! Every exported entry point of this crate (and every callback invocation
36//! inside an exported frame) is panic-contained by the UniFFI 0.31.2
37//! scaffolding itself; [`crate::panic_boundary`] documents the containment
38//! map, which is why this crate adds no redundant `catch_unwind` layer of
39//! its own, and holds the crate's `unwrap`/`expect` policy for FFI-reachable
40//! code.
41
42// The scaffolding generated from the UDL builds its namespace metadata in
43// large const arrays (uniffi 0.31's codegen pattern); the attribute cannot
44// scope into the machine-generated macro expansion, so the lint is allowed
45// crate-wide.
46#![allow(clippy::large_const_arrays)]
47// No `unwrap`/`expect` in FFI-reachable code: the deny is lifted for the
48// test builds (the crate's own unit-test modules and the integration-test
49// crates, which rustc also compiles with `cfg(test)`), where the
50// conventional assertion shorthands are honest. The production denials are
51// upheld by the invariants documented in [`crate::panic_boundary`], whose
52// helpers hold the crate's only justifications for the remaining,
53// deliberately panic-expressing sites.
54#![cfg_attr(not(test), deny(clippy::expect_used, clippy::unwrap_used))]

56// The wasm2 player's runtime helpers (`__ubrn_alloc`/`__ubrn_free` and the
57// panic-hook installation). Nothing in this crate calls into them, so the
58// dependency must be referenced for its side effects: without this line
59// rustc drops the unused rlib and the player fails at init with
60// `required export "__ubrn_alloc" not found in wasm module`.
