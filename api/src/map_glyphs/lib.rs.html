1//! Glyph support for the symbol/text pipeline (M9 slice S2): the Mapbox glyph
2//! protobuf reader, a byte-bounded range cache and an `R8Unorm` atlas packer.
3//!
4//! The crate is pure byte parsing and CPU-side packing. Its only runtime
5//! dependencies are [`thiserror`] and the workspace's dependency-free
6//! protobuf wire reader (`map-vector-tiles`), so it builds on native targets
7//! and on `wasm32-unknown-unknown` alike. Nothing here draws, touches a GPU
8//! pipeline or places labels; the renderer's text pass is slice S3.
9//!
10//! # Scope: Latin only
11//!
12//! The reader parses whichever codepoints a producer serves, and the atlas
13//! packs the Latin ranges first, but there is no shaping, bidirectional
14//! ordering or ligature handling. Complex-script text (Arabic, Indic, …) is
15//! explicitly out of scope until a shaping dependency is justified.
16//!
17//! Entry points: [`decode_glyph_font_stack`] turns a fetched glyph range into
18//! a [`GlyphFontStack`], [`GlyphRangeCache`] retains decoded ranges under a
19//! byte and entry ceiling, and [`pack_glyph_atlas`] lays glyphs out on one
20//! `R8Unorm` page with per-glyph texture rectangles.
