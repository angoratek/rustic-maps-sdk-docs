1//! OpenStreetMap US tileservice implementation of
2//! [`geospatial_traits::VectorTileProvider`], the first provider behind the
3//! M9 vector-tile seam (PLAN.md M9, owner direction 2026-10-29).
4//!
5//! # Service facts (recorded 2026-10-29 from the service's own pages)
6//!
7//! - Service: the [OpenStreetMap US tileservice](https://openstreetmap.us/our-work/tileservice/),
8//!   operated by OpenStreetMap US (the US chapter of the OSM Foundation).
9//!   Vector tilesets are listed at
10//!   <https://tiles.openstreetmap.us/vector/>; this implementation serves the
11//!   **Sourdough** tileset, osmus's homegrown schema, documented at
12//!   <https://sourdough.osm.fyi/schema/> and mirrored in the TileJSON at
13//!   <https://tiles.openstreetmap.us/vector/sourdough.json>.
14//! - Tile URL template (verbatim from the TileJSON `tiles` array):
15//!   `https://tiles.openstreetmap.us/vector/sourdough/{z}/{x}/{y}.mvt`.
16//! - Zoom span (TileJSON `minzoom`/`maxzoom`): 0 through 15; scheme `xyz`.
17//! - Wire format (live probe, 2026-10-29): `application/x-protobuf`
18//!   MVT protobuf, served gzip-wrapped (`Content-Encoding: gzip`); the
19//!   provider records the encoding it received instead of decompressing.
20//! - Attribution (usage policy, <https://tiles.openstreetmap.us/usage-policy/>):
21//!   OSM-derived tiles require **all** of `Tiles by OSM US` (linked to
22//!   <https://tiles.openstreetmap.us>), `© OpenStreetMap` (linked to
23//!   <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>), and — on OpenMapTiles-schema
24//!   tilesets — `© OpenMapTiles` (linked to <https://openmaptiles.org>);
25//!   surfaced on the provider through
26//!   [`geospatial_traits::VectorTileProvider::source_descriptor`].
27//! - Usage policy: the service is free for low-volume, non-commercial use
28//!   (Development/Starter tiers; commercial or higher-volume use requires an
29//!   approved partnership application). Requests must carry accurate
30//!   identifying headers ([`vector_tiles::OSMUS_USER_AGENT_TEXT_PREFIX`]),
31//!   must not be spoofed, must honor `Cache-Control` responses, and must
32//!   back off on HTTP 429. The fixtures-first tests of this crate and of its
33//!   consumers keep the SDK from ever downloading tiles during tests; the
34//!   reference apps' posture stays low-volume non-commercial.
35//!
36//! # Crate posture
37//!
38//! Like `mapquest-provider`, this crate sits outside the wasm closure: a
39//! native-only network provider (reqwest + the crate-owned rate limiter).
40//! The wasm path gains vector tiles through its own seam in a later M9
41//! slice; the contract in `geospatial-traits` is the wasm-clean part.
